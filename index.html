🐣 How to Take Care of Baby Chickens (Chicks)

Creme Legbar Chickens: From Baby to Adult

Creme Legbars are a beautiful and popular breed known for their sky-blue eggs and friendly personalities. Here's a visual guide to their growth stages:

Newly hatched Creme Legbar chick. Growing into a juvenile stage. Fully grown adult Creme Legbar hen.

1. Set Up a Brooder

A brooder is a warm, safe space for your baby chicks to grow. Use a plastic bin, cardboard box, or a small animal cage lined with pine shavings. Avoid newspaper as it's too slippery for chicks' feet.

  • Temperature: Start at 95°F (35°C) for the first week.
  • Reduce temperature by 5°F each week until fully feathered.
  • Use a heat lamp or brooder plate. Ensure it's safely secured!

2. Feed and Water

Chicks need proper nutrition and clean water 24/7:

  • Feed chick starter feed with at least 18–20% protein.
  • Use a shallow dish or chick waterer to avoid drowning.
  • Add small stones or marbles in the water tray for safety.

3. Keep the Brooder Clean

Cleanliness helps prevent disease:

  • Change bedding every few days or when soiled.
  • Wipe down feeders and waterers daily.
  • Watch for signs of pasty butt (poop sticking to vent) — clean gently with warm water.

4. Socialize and Monitor

Chicks are social and curious creatures:

  • Talk to them gently and handle with clean hands.
  • Watch for bullying or injury.
  • Ensure all chicks are eating, drinking, and active.

5. Prepare for the Coop

At 6–8 weeks, chicks can move outside if weather permits and they're fully feathered:

  • Ensure the coop is predator-proof, dry, and draft-free.
  • Provide roosts, feeders, and waterers sized for growing birds.
  • Start mixing in grower feed as they mature.

Final tip: Chicks are fragile but resilient with good care. Take it one step at a time and enjoy the peeping! 🐤
